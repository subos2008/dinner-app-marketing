# Catchphrase idea: "Yummy experience"

*2026-02-28*

---

Double meaning:

1. **Literally yummy** — we're sending people for dinner. Food. Restaurants.
2. **Figuratively yummy** — a delicious experience. Something you want more of.

"Yummy" as a word: warm, playful, slightly childish in a good way, not corporate, not cringe. Could work as a recurring brand word.

Possible uses:
- "Your next yummy experience is a Wednesday away"
- "Yummy food. Yummy people. Yummy Wednesday."
- Hashtag: #YummyExperience
- CTA: "Book a yummy Wednesday"

Needs testing — does it land or does it feel too cutesy? Might depend on segment. Could work well for the lighter/playful end of the tone spectrum.
