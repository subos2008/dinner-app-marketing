# Segment Strategy

## Launch Context
- **Phase 1:** Manchester (home turf, can iterate fast)
- **Phase 2:** London (large, English-speaking, high density of target profiles)
- **Phase 3:** Major US cities (NYC, LA, Chicago, SF, etc.)
- **Age range:** 25-35 primary, stretching to 45 as secondary
- **Large cities matter** — density of strangers, transient populations, higher loneliness rates

## Segment Types

### Life-Situation Segments
Defined by where someone is in life. These are the primary targeting dimensions.

| Segment | Ease of targeting | Willingness to act | Volume | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [The Transplant](the-transplant/profile.md) | High (location change data) | High (actively looking) | Medium | **#1 — Launch with this** |
| [The Explorer](the-explorer/profile.md) | High (interest + location data) | High (time-limited, urgent) | Low-Medium | **#2 — High-value early adopter** |
| [The Outgrower](the-outgrower/profile.md) | Medium (interest-based) | Medium (needs the right nudge) | High | **#3 — Scale with this** |
| [The Quiet One](the-quiet-one/profile.md) | Low (passive, hard to identify) | Low (high barrier) | High | **#4 — Content/brand play** |

### Lifestyle Segments
Defined by how someone lives. These cross-cut the life-situation segments and can be layered on top.

| Segment | Ease of targeting | Willingness to act | Volume | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [The Sober One](the-sober-one/profile.md) | High (interest + community signals) | High (underserved, specific need) | Medium | **Run alongside launch — strong differentiator** |
| [The Plant-Based One](the-plant-based-one/profile.md) | High (clear interest signals) | High (identity-driven) | Medium | **Run alongside launch — easy to target, partnership potential** |

### Layering
This is a strength. It means we can run:
- Broad campaigns: "New to Manchester? Meet your people."
- Layered campaigns: "New to Manchester and sober? Meet your people."
- Lifestyle-specific campaigns: "A vegan dinner with 5 people who actually get it."

This mirrors how our matching actually works — multiple dimensions, not just one.

## Ad Strategy Implications

**The Transplant** is the launch segment. Easy to find on Meta, high intent, low persuasion needed. The product basically sells itself to this person — "dinner with 5 people who are also new here" is a no-brainer.

**The Explorer** is a high-value early adopter. Smaller pool but built-in urgency (they're leaving eventually), strong word-of-mouth in online nomad communities, and natural multi-city repeat potential as we expand. They're also easy to target — remote work and travel interests light up on Meta.

**The Outgrower** is the scale segment. Bigger pool, but needs more sophisticated messaging. They don't think of themselves as lonely — you have to frame it as growth, not fixing a problem.

**The Quiet One** won't respond to ads that say "meet new people!" — they've heard it before. This segment needs trust-building content: testimonials, stories, a brand that feels safe. Less performance marketing, more organic/brand.

**The Sober One** and **The Plant-Based One** are lifestyle segments that layer on top. They can run as standalone campaigns or combined with life-situation targeting ("New to Manchester and sober?"). These are strong differentiators — they prove we actually use our matching questions, unlike competitors. Both have active, vocal communities that share resources, so word-of-mouth potential is high.

## Secondary Segments (Future / Worth Watching)

### The Life Changer
Post-breakup, post-divorce, new parent whose friend group shifted. Social circle disrupted by circumstance. Similar to The Transplant but didn't move — their life moved around them.

### The Expat / International
Non-native English speaker in a new country. Extra isolation layer — cultural and linguistic. Huge in London and US cities. Could be a powerful niche.

## Open Questions
- What's the price point? Affects which segments are viable (The Transplant is often early-career, price-sensitive)
- Do we lead with "dinner" or "connection"? The Transplant cares about the activity. The Outgrower cares about the people. The Quiet One cares about the safety.
- Manchester-specific: any local cultural angles worth leaning into?
