## Overall Assessment

### Top 3 Strongest Ads

1. **The Wednesday Night Problem — Variant B** (5/5) — The lead ad. "The flat's quiet enough to hear the fridge" is sensory and specific. "The evenings haven't caught up to the ambition" is the line of the whole campaign. Everything earns its place.

2. **The Google Search — Variant A** (5/5) — The most universally relatable hook. Everyone who's moved cities has done this search. "Stop googling. Start going." is the punchiest CTA in the set.

3. **Nobody Tells You — Variant A** (5/5) — The best storytelling. The month-by-month cadence builds inevitability. "Month nine" as a specific beat is the kind of detail that makes someone think "they actually understand."

### Top 3 Standout Lines

1. **"The evenings haven't caught up to the ambition."** — This is the Transplant campaign in one line. Works as a hook, body line, or standalone.

2. **"Stop googling. Start going."** — CTA that doubles as a campaign tagline. Test as a standalone hook.

3. **"Not randomly. Not algorithmically in a way nobody can explain. Specifically, deliberately, based on what you told us."** — The best product description in the set. Builds trust through precision. Reuse across segments.

### Biggest Gap

**The empathy work surfaced strong social proof angles that aren't fully used.** The emotional journey's "after dinner" section — "I didn't find my best friends. I found proof that I can still do this" — is powerful but only appears in concepts, not in finished ads. Consider writing a dedicated social proof ad using the first-person after-dinner voice. The "two other people at my table had also just moved here" angle is also unused and could be a strong retargeting ad.

### Rewrite Recommendations

No ads scored below 3. Minor fixes:

- **Google Search B:** Replace `[city]` with the specific city name for each deployment market
- **Google Search Reels:** Add a clear CTA ("Book a dinner") alongside "Delete the search history"
- **Real Questions A:** Trim the last paragraph — "It's called Come Join Us" is unnecessary, the CTA handles the brand intro
- **Wednesday Night Problem A:** Tighten "Come Join Us changes one night a week" → "Come Join Us is one night a week" or similar

### Lines from Archived Copy (ad-copy.old.md) Worth Testing

The original vibes-driven copy had some strong lines the process-driven copy didn't produce:

1. **"your mum is still your most recent call"** — Specific gut-punch detail. Consider as a Reel hook or feed ad variant.
2. **"You know your flatmate and your team at work. That's it. That's the list."** — Punchy Reel cadence. The rhythm is effective.
3. **"Nobody's getting dragged to a £60-a-head place when they said £20"** — Concrete budget proof with real numbers. The new copy mentions budget matching abstractly — consider adding specific price points.
4. **"Not your Myers-Briggs type. Not your spirit animal."** — More specific competitor digs than just "star sign." Could strengthen The Real Questions concept.

These could be folded into future iterations or used as additional test variants.
