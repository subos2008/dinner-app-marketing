# Ad Copy: The Quiet One

*Genuinely isolated. High barrier. Needs safety and trust.*

## Feed Ads

**Ad 1 — Permission**
> You don't have to be outgoing to want connection.
>
> You don't have to be "good at" social situations.
>
> You just have to show up. We handle the rest — matching you with 5 people over dinner based on who you actually are, not who you perform to be.
>
> One dinner. Low pressure. Real people.

**Ad 2 — Honesty**
> This isn't a networking event. There's no name tags. Nobody's going to ask what you do for a living within 30 seconds.
>
> It's dinner. With 5 people we've matched you with because you actually have things in common.
>
> If you've been thinking about putting yourself out there — this is a good way to start.

**Ad 3 — Not Another App**
> We know. You've tried the meetup apps. The "friend-finding" apps. The awkward drinks where everyone already knows each other.
>
> This is different. 6 people. One table. Matched on real things — not algorithms pretending to know you.
>
> You don't have to be perfect at this. You just have to book.

**Ad 4 — The Safety Net**
> Here's everything we do so you don't have to worry:
>
> We match you with people your age. We balance the genders at the table. We pick a restaurant that's been vetted — no loud music, no chaos, good vibes. We send icebreaker questions so the conversation has somewhere to start.
>
> A real person reviews every group before you get your confirmation.
>
> You just show up. Wednesday. That's it.

**Ad 5 — The Surprise**
> You don't know who's coming. That's the point.
>
> No profiles to stalk. No photos to judge. No "should I go?" spiral.
>
> 5 people. Matched on the things that matter. A restaurant we picked for you. Icebreakers to get things going.
>
> Sometimes the best people in your life are the ones you didn't choose from a menu.
