# The Explorer

**Segment type:** Life-situation

**Who:** Digital nomad or someone on an extended trip (1-6 months in a city). Has a laptop, a coworking membership or a cafe routine, and zero local friends. They chose freedom but didn't budget for how isolating it can be.

**Age skew:** 25-38

**Emotional state:** Loves the lifestyle, won't admit the trade-off. Instagram looks amazing — the reality is eating alone most nights in a city full of people they'll never speak to. Craves real conversation, not another hostel bar or coworking "networking drinks."

**Trigger:** Third dinner alone in a row. Or realising they've had more Slack conversations than real ones this week. The novelty of a new city wearing off and the loneliness settling in.

**What they'd tell a friend:** "It's amazing here, honestly. I just... wouldn't mind knowing some actual people."

**Meta targeting signals:**
- Interests: remote work, digital nomad, coworking, travel
- Follows accounts like Nomad List, Remote Year, travel creators
- Location doesn't match "hometown" — Meta knows this
- Recently arrived in city (similar to Transplant but temporary)
- May follow food/restaurant content in current city
- Age 25-38

**Messaging hook:** You didn't travel this far to eat alone.

**Why this segment matters:**
- High willingness to act — they're time-limited, so urgency is built in. No "I'll do it next month."
- Great word-of-mouth potential — nomads are networked online. One good experience gets shared in communities, Slack groups, Twitter/X.
- Natural repeat customers across cities — if we expand to multiple cities, this person books a dinner in every new place they land.
- Low friction — they're already used to showing up somewhere new and figuring it out. The social bravery barrier is lower.

**Priority:** #2 — High-value early adopter. Smaller pool but built-in urgency, strong word-of-mouth, natural multi-city repeat potential.
