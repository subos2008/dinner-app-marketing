# The Hippy

**Segment type:** Lifestyle (cross-cuts life-situation segments)

**Who:** Into conscious living — meditation, yoga, sound baths, gong baths, workshops, breathwork, cacao ceremonies, retreats. Likely sober or sober-curious. Not necessarily woo-woo about it — some are pragmatic wellness people, some are deeply spiritual. What they share: they've built a lifestyle around mindfulness and intentional living, and they struggle to find their people in everyday social settings.

**Age skew:** 25-42

**Emotional state:** Feels like they've "woken up" to something most people around them haven't. Not in an arrogant way — in a lonely way. They've changed how they live, what they consume, how they spend their time. But their existing friends still want to go to the pub, still eat without thinking, still live on autopilot. They're not judgemental (or try not to be), but they're craving a table where they don't have to explain why they don't drink, why they meditate, or why they spent Saturday at a breathwork session instead of brunch.

**Trigger:** Coming back from a workshop or retreat feeling expanded and connected, then going back to their normal social life where nobody asks about it. Or: wanting to go out on a Wednesday but knowing the only options involve bars.

**What they'd tell a friend:** "I just want to meet people who are on the same wavelength. Not necessarily doing the exact same things — just people who get it."

**Meta targeting signals:**
- Interests: meditation, yoga, sound healing, breathwork, conscious living, spiritual growth, plant medicine (broad), retreats
- Follows accounts like Wim Hof, Eckhart Tolle, Ram Dass, Gabor Maté, Russell Brand (wellness era), local retreat centres, sound bath practitioners
- Engages with: sober curious content, mindfulness apps (Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer), holistic health, energy healing
- Likely overlaps with sober and plant-based segments
- May follow local wellness events, cacao ceremonies, kirtan nights
- Age 25-42

**Messaging hook:** A dinner with people who actually get it.

**Why this segment matters:**
- **Sober matching is the unlock** — many in this segment don't drink. Our alcohol matching means they'll be at a table where sobriety is the default, not the exception.
- **Interest matching (future)** — when interest-based matching goes live, this segment benefits most. "What do you want more of?" answers like meditation, outdoors, sober events are exactly their profile.
- **Highly targetable on Meta** — the wellness/spiritual community has clear interest signals and engaged audiences.
- **Community-driven word of mouth** — this audience shares. One good experience gets posted in WhatsApp groups, shared at workshops, mentioned in circles. They're natural amplifiers.
- **Cross-cuts sober and plant-based** — significant overlap with The Sober One and The Plant-Based One, but the primary identity is the conscious living lifestyle, not just diet or drinking.

**Priority:** Run alongside lifestyle segments — strong overlap with sober and plant-based but distinct enough to deserve its own creative.
